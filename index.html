01 — About Based in Indonesia
UTC+7 · working worldwide

A decade of code taught me how. Four years in production taught me why it matters.

I'm Ivan Azhari, a software engineer with more than ten years of programming behind me and four years of professional, real-world delivery. What began as curiosity became a craft: reading systems, shaping interfaces, and shipping work that people actually use.

I work across the stack — front-end surfaces, APIs, data models, automation, and the unglamorous glue that keeps a product running. I like small teams, clear constraints, and code the next person can read without needing a meeting.

The work is not finished when it compiles. It's finished when it is deployed, observed, and understood.
