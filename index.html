01 — About Based in Indonesia
UTC+7 · working worldwide

A decade of code taught me how. Four years in production taught me why it matters.

I'm Ivan Azhari, a Python programmer and software architect with more than ten years of programming behind me and four years of professional delivery. What began as curiosity became a craft: reading systems, shaping data, and shipping work that people actually use.

My work lives where business rules meet infrastructure — FastAPI services, relational schemas, automation playbooks, and the interfaces that make them usable. I've built security automation for one of Indonesia's largest banks, connected business software to Odoo ERP, and taken my own products from an empty schema to a working interface.

Architecture, to me, is deciding where change is allowed to happen. Small boundaries, honest contracts, and code the next person can read without needing a meeting.
